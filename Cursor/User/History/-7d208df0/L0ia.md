The exocortex is a theoretical concept that allows you to augment your brain with an external brain.

### Objectives for Our Exocortex Implementation

- Automate all repetitive tasks.
- Serve as a partial replacement for you when you are busy or incapacitated.
- Remain loyal and confidential (i.e., it won't disclose your private information).
- Possess comprehensive knowledge about you.

### Importance of the Exocortex

We are currently on a trajectory towards Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) in the coming years. ASI refers to an intelligent system that surpasses the collective intelligence of all humans. There are two potential paths for humanity in the evolving AI landscape:

1. Relying on large foundational AGI models hosted on centralized servers by companies like OpenAI. In this scenario, AI continues to advance while humans do not physically evolve, leading to an increasing gap between human and AI capabilities.
   
2. Augmenting ourselves to evolve alongside these highly intelligent models. This vision aligns with the goals of companies like Neuralink, which aim to assist disabled individuals in regaining mobility or vision in the short term, while aspiring to evolve humans into cyborgs in the long term.
