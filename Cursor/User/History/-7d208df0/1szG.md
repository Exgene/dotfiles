The exocortex is a theoretical concept that allows you to augment your brain with an external brain.

## Objectives of Our Exocortex Implementation

- **Automate all repetitive tasks.**
- **Partially replace you** when you are busy (or incapacitated).
- Ensure the exocortex is **faithful to you** (i.e., it won't disclose your private information).
- The exocortex will possess **comprehensive knowledge about you**.

## Importance of the Exocortex

We are currently on a trajectory towards Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) in the coming years. ASI refers to an intelligent system that surpasses the collective intelligence of all humans. 

There are two potential paths for humanity in the evolving AI landscape:

1. **Centralized AGI Models**: Large foundational AGI models operated by companies like OpenAI, which evolve independently of human physical evolution. This path risks widening the gap between humans and AI.
  
2. **Human Augmentation**: Enhancing ourselves to evolve alongside highly intelligent models. This vision aligns with the goals of companies like Neuralink, which aim to assist disabled individuals in regaining mobility or vision, with a long-term vision of evolving humans into cyborgs.
