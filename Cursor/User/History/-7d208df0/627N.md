Theoretical concept where you can append you brain with an external brain.

What do we want our implementation of exocortex to do?

- Automate all repetitive tasks
- Should be able to partially replace you when you are busy (or die)
- The exocortex should be faithful to you (ie. won't snitch on you)
- The exocortex will know everything about you

Why is this important?

- Currently are are on a path towards AGI and ASI in the coming years. ASI is an intelligent system that is more
  intelligent than all humans combined. The way we see this is there are only two paths that humans can take in the coming AI
  future. 1. You have these large foundational AGI models that run in some centralized server by companies such as OpenAI etc.
  AI keeps evolving while humans don't physically evolve. If we go with this path the gap between humans and AI will keep increasing. 2. You augment yourself to evolve along with the highly intelligent models.
  This concept is the vision of companies such as neuralink, who have a short term goal of helping disabled people walk
  or blind people see and have a long term goal of evolving humans into cyborgs.
